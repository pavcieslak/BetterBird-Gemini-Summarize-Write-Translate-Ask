Gemini Mail Assistant

Summarize or translate emails, draft replies and get writing help from Gemini, on demand.

Gemini API

Create a free key in Google AI Studio. It is stored only in this Betterbird profile and sent only to Google's API.
The default gemini-flash-latest always points to Google's current fast model. “Load models” lists what your key can use.

Defaults

Added to every request. Useful for your name, sign-off style or preferred spelling.

Advanced

Lower is more literal (good for summaries and translation), higher is more varied.
Longer emails are cut off to keep requests fast and cheap.

Privacy: nothing is sent until you press a button in the Gemini popup. Then the text of the email you are working on (and, for replies, the original message) is sent to Google's Gemini API using your key. Google's terms and data-use policy for the API apply; the free tier may use submitted content to improve Google products, so consider a paid key for confidential mail.
